# ADR-0008: Printed belt clip, printed segmented 1U panel, snap-arm battery door
Status: accepted. 2026-10-31.
- **Context:** PK-11's 25 mm left no room for a clip; a 482.6 mm panel exceeds the owner's printer, a Bambu A1 mini (180 × 180 × 180 mm, open frame, PETG on textured PEI, no ABS/ASA). The owner prefers printed parts to bought ones (R15).
- **Decision:** PK-11 limits the printed body only (96 × 62 × 22.8 mm). The belt clip is a separate PETG print (3 mm root tapering to 2.4 mm, ≥ 4 mm mouth, lead-in lip, printed on its side, peak strain ~1 %), on 2× M2 screws into heat-set inserts in 7 mm bosses (ring 1.9 mm, floor 1.6 mm). The 1U panel is 3 segments (≤ 166 mm, 5 mm bed margin) joined by 2 printed backing strips with 4× M3 bolts + inserts each; ears 5 mm. The battery door gets two snap arms; nothing needs supports.
- **Consequences:** `SPEC.md` PK-11 reworded; BOM unchanged (clip in "enclosure + clip", no panel line). Tests check the 180 mm bed, overhangs, seams and cutout margins, and slice with a 180 mm PETG profile. Clip fatigue life and snap-arm fit need a first print; PETG creep may need a thicker arm.
- **Requirements check:** R15 ✓ (all printed) · R7 ✓ (door and cell swap without tools) · R4 ✓ (no user controls) · R9 ✓ (1U, 8 XLR, rack-ear holes) · robustness: partial (printed clip fatigues sooner than steel; inserts and 5 mm ears mitigate).
