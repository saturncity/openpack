# ADR-0022: The pack holds its frame lead with a PI loop on HFCLKAUDIO, fed by the net core's latch margin
Status: accepted. 2026-11-01. Issue #97. Follows ADR-0020 (frame clock phased to the latch) and #98 (latch 20 µs before TXEN). Reuses ADR-0013's PI loop (`op_clk.c`).
- **Context:** On silicon the frame clock is I2S RXPTRUPD (48 samples), clocked by HFCLKAUDIO. Its PLL cannot hit 12.288 MHz: FREQ 39846 is +0.371 ppm, and one FREQ step is 3311 ppb. Against TIMER0 (net core latch) the 100 µs lead drifts 1.34 ms/h. Open loop, the pack skips one frame about every 40 min (unit test: first skip after 2427 s). Between skips the margin sweeps from 0 to 1 ms. bsim has one crystal per device, so `-xo_drift` cannot separate the two clocks.
- **Decision:** (1) The pack is paced from I2S RXPTRUPD (`op_audio_i2s.c`). A TIMER1 one-shot at the synced phase starts I2S. Without I2S (bsim, WAV backend) TIMER1 at 16 MHz stands in, free-running with absolute compares (the bsim TIMER rounds each CLEAR-relative period up to a whole µs). (2) Every `OP_PACK_LEAD_REPORT_FRAMES` (100) frames the net core sends the window's latch margins (`OP_LEAD_LEN`, same endpoint). The first window is 10 frames. (3) `op_lead` takes the earliest frame's margin, which encode time and load jitter cannot bias. The first report is the reference. A PI loop (kp 2000 ppb/µs, ki 100 ppb/µs/report, poles at z = 0.9, ~1 s) gives the rate correction. A first-order sigma-delta dithers it per frame between whole FREQ steps. (4) Test-only `-op_fclk_ppb` / `OP_TEST_FCLK_PPB` offsets the stand-in's rate; `-op_lead_open` opens the loop.
- **Requirements check:**

| R-id | A: PI on HFCLKAUDIO from the latch margin (chosen) | B: open loop, drop a frame every ~45 min | C: re-sync (restart I2S) when the margin runs low |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 no dropouts | ✓ 0 skips/h at +0.371 and ±40 ppm (bsim, unit test) | ✗ one PLC event per ~45 min per pack | partial: a restart glitch per re-sync |
| R3 latency | ✓ lead held at 100 ± 3 µs | ✗ margin sweeps 0..1 ms: +0..1 ms latency | partial: sweeps between re-syncs |
| R13 sim first | ✓ same loop on bsim (stand-in) and silicon | ✓ | ✓ |

- **Consequences:** bsim `tests/bsim/sched/lead.sh` runs the real firmware at +0.371 and ±40 ppm: 300 s per PR, 1 h nightly, plus an open-loop negative control. Over 1 simulated hour each, it gives 0 skips and 0 repeats. The margin settles at 100 µs (±1) within 60 s; the peak transient is 16 µs at ±40 ppm and 1 µs at +0.371. Open loop, −40 ppm repeats within 2 s. `fw/tests/pack_lead` closes the same loop around a modelled plant for 1 h per case. The I2S capture path (pins P0.24/P0.23/P0.20, ADR-0012) is first exercised on silicon in Phase 5. Phase 5 also checks that FREQ writes once per ms do not disturb the PLL.
