# ADR-0006: Front end is one path, +6 dB, PCM1840 DRE
Status: accepted (default Q7, confirmed by `hw/spice/preamp.cir`, `tests/test_spice_preamp.py`). 2026-10-31.
- **Context:** PK-2/PK-3: 0.6 Vrms max in (R2), ≤ 1 µV A-weighted input-referred (R5), 5 V single supply, 1 kΩ mic, 5 V bias via 2.2 kΩ, PCM1840 2 Vrms FS. Model: generic op-amp macromodel with OPA1656 datasheet numbers (GBW 53 MHz, en 4.3 nV/√Hz, 50 mV rail swing), not TI's model (needs a login); re-run with it when supplied.
- **Decision:** One path: AC-coupled non-inverting x2 (330 Ω/330 Ω), mid-rail bias, into the PCM1840 with DRE on. No second gain path switched in v1 firmware (schematic may still carry it, Q7). Numbers: noise 0.94 µV A-wtd (1 kΩ source and 2.2 kΩ bias included; Rf/Rg 1 kΩ gave 1.02 µV, so 330 Ω); gain 2.71 dB flat 20 Hz–40 kHz within 0.05 dB (the 2.2 kΩ/1 kΩ bias divider costs 3.3 dB of the +6 dB); at 0.6 Vrms output is 1.34–3.66 V, 1.3 V clear of the rails, 1.2 Vrms into a 2 Vrms ADC.
- **Consequences:** Noise margin is only 6 %: the 5 V rail must be quiet (supply noise is not modelled) and the real op-amp 1/f noise is not modelled; re-check against TI's model and on the bench. Hotter mics have > 3 dB headroom to spare; quieter ones are the risk, so DRE does the rest.
- **Requirements check:** R2 ✓ (no clipping at 0.6 Vrms) · R5 ✓ (0.94 µV ≤ 1 µV) · R6 ✓ (5 V/2.2 kΩ bias, 1 kΩ source) · R12 ✓ (one op-amp, no switch) · R4 ✓ (no gain control).
