# ADR-0016: R1 under UK rules: directional rack antennas, an install rule, and an 8th gap carrier at 2482 MHz
Status: accepted (owner decision A, 2026-11-01). Issues #89, #26. Amends ADR-0015 (channel plan, R1 stop-and-ask). Numbers: `python3 audit.py` Audit 4 and Audit 5; study `docs/analysis/r1-uk-isolation.md`.
- **Context:** ADR-0015 left R1 failing at UK power (9 dBm EIRP). At the Q3 geometry only 2 of 8 packs passed: 5 gap carriers 11 MHz from Wi-Fi 1/6/11 need 12.7 dB more isolation, and pack 1 had no gap carrier. The #89 study found that two directional rack antennas, with the audience Wi-Fi behind them, close that gap, if the install rule is followed. It assumed a 15 dB front-to-back ratio. Pack 1 also needs an 8th gap carrier. The only one left at 3 MHz spacing is 2482, 1.5 MHz below the 2483.5 band edge.
- **2482 legality (EN 300 328 V2.2.2, clause text checked):** 4.3.2.7 requires the 99 % OCB "within the band given in table 1" (2400–2483.5). It is measured at normal conditions only (5.4.7.1). Ideal BLE-2M GFSK has a 2.06 MHz OCB. A measured nRF52840 EN 300 328 report (ISL-18LR094E328) gives 1.84 MHz, top edge 2480.978 at 2480. Add +50 ppm crystal and the ±0.12 MHz lab uncertainty, and the top edge at 2482 is 2483.28 MHz. It would fail only above a 2.51 MHz OCB. 4.3.2.8 Figure 3 limits are −10 dBm/MHz EIRP from 2483.5 to +BW and −20 from +BW to +2BW; 4.3.2.9 sets −30 dBm/MHz above that. The nRF5340 PS v1.3.1 gives PRF1,2 −25 dBc (1st adjacent, 2 Mbps) and PRF2,2 −50 dBc. At 9 dBm that bounds the segments at −16 / −41 / −41 dBm/MHz, so the margins are 6 / 21 / 11 dB. The nRF21540 in the TX path runs at +7 dBm, 15 dB below its +22 dBm maximum. GFSK has a constant envelope, so the FEM adds no regrowth. **Verdict: compliant. Adopted for UK/EU only.** The US stays at 2480: 2483.5–2500 is a restricted band under 15.205.
- **Real antenna:** the **L-com HyperLink HG2409P-NF** is an 8 dBi flat patch, beamwidth 75° H / 65° V, N-female on a 12 in lead. ITM Components (UK) sells it at £72.12. Its datasheet states no F/B number, only polar plots. Read off them: −18 dB at 180° (V plane), but rear lobes reach **−9 dB** at about 120–135°. An audience spread behind the antenna sees those lobes, so the real rejection is **8.8 dB, not 14.4**. At the Q3 geometry (Wi-Fi 2 m away) that gives **3 of 8** (only 2476/2479/2482). Distance makes up the rest. With 3 dB margin, Wi-Fi must be **≥ 4.4 m behind or beside** an antenna (study: 3 m / 4 m) and ≥ 12.2 m in front. bsim (c) at 40 % (`--rx-gain 8 --wifi-gain -7.6 --near-att 43.2`, 20 s, 2026-11-01): packs 2–8 lose **0.000 %, TD on and TD off**. Pack 1 fails there (8.1 %) only because bsim cannot transmit above 2480, so it runs the in-band US pair. On 2482 it is credited by equivalence: 20 MHz from Wi-Fi 11, while 2476/2479 lose 0 % in every run.
- **Decision:** UK plan (`host/channel_plan.yaml`): pack 1 → 2414/2482, so every pack has a gap carrier. US plan: `host/channel_plan_us.yaml`, the ADR-0015 plan. Validator, pack `chan` and Kconfig cap fB by region (2482 uk/eu, 2480 us/jp); the receiver's `pair` accepts 2482. Rack: two HG2409P-NF, each on L-com CA3N050 (400-series, 15.2 m, £61.07) plus an LCCA30301-FT1.5 BNC-male/N-female jumper (£37.09) into the RK-1 BNC. That is 4.2 dB of the 6 dB RK-2 budget, **£340.56 per rack ex VAT** (a purchase: stop-and-ask, Phase 5). Install rule (`docs/human/antenna-install.md`): front of stage, aimed upstage, **≥ 6.0 m above the stage floor**. That keeps actors ≥ 4 m away (near-far) and phones ≥ 4.4 m away even on a flat floor. Venue Wi-Fi on 1/6/11, not 13.

- **Consequences:** R1 now depends on placement, so a venue that cannot follow the rule falls back to TD on (ADR-0017) and, after it, hybrid 1M. If the venue runs Wi-Fi 13, packs 1 and 6–8 lose their gap copy. Phase 5 measures four things: the real 99 % OBW and the 2483.5–2487.6 MHz emissions at 2482 on a pack (bench spectrum analyser), the HG2409P rear lobes, and real phones' leakage at 11–17 MHz. If the 2482 OBW measures over 2.51 MHz, the fallback is the US-plan pair for pack 1 with TD on (4.58 ms, R1 partial for that pack). No 2481 alternative works: it sits 2 MHz from 2479, and 2 MHz neighbours collide (#76).
