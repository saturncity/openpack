Phase: 2 of 6 (firmware in the simulator)
Done since last update: Time diversity (resending each sound a moment later) is now a real per-pack setting (#91). A pack is switched with `td on` over its USB cable, and the receiver works it out by itself. In the simulator, a pack with no clear channel under 40 % audience Wi-Fi drops from 21.9 % lost sound to 8.9 %. At 10 % Wi-Fi it drops from 1.6 % to 0.09 %. With it on, sound arrives 4.58 thousandths of a second after the mic (limit 5). It is still off by default (2.65).
Next: finish the remaining simulator tests (#27).
Waiting on human: (1) when Phase 5 starts, buy 2 antennas and their cables, £340.56 ex VAT from ITM Components UK; the list and links are in issue #92. (2) Install KiCad (the circuit-board program): type `sudo dnf install kicad`. Not urgent, needed before board layout.
Risks: The real antenna rejects sound from behind less well than the study assumed (about 9 instead of 15 "decibels", a measure of loudness). So the rule is stricter: Wi-Fi gear must stay at least 4.5 m from the antennas (not 3 m), and the antennas must be at least 6 m up. If a venue can't do that, switch the nearest packs to time diversity.
