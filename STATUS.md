Phase: 2 of 6 (firmware in the simulator)
Done since last update: The firmware skeleton now runs on both chips' cores, with the sound compressor (codec) and the audio in/out layer in place. The pack's radio timing works in the simulator: it is off by 0 to 1 millionth of a second (5 allowed), and no sound packets were lost in a 10-second run. The receiver is built too: it locks onto a pack in 1.3 thousandths of a second, locks again after the pack restarts, and picks the better of its two antennas. It also answers the settings and status commands from the computer.
