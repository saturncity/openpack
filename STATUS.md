Phase: 2 of 6 (firmware in the simulator)
Done since last update: Clock sync between the body pack and the rack. All five simulated tests pass with the new UK radio plan. UK radio rules are worked out and built in. The delay from microphone to output is fixed at 2.3 ms. Both circuit-board schematics are done. A UK parts list is done. Time diversity (resending each sound a moment later) is now a real per-pack setting (#91). A pack is switched with `td on` over its USB cable, and the receiver works it out by itself. In the simulator, a pack with no clear channel under 40 % audience Wi-Fi drops from 21.9 % lost sound to 8.9 %. At 10 % Wi-Fi it drops from 1.6 % to 0.09 %. With it on, sound arrives 4.58 thousandths of a second after the mic (limit 5). It is still off by default (2.65).
Next: the backplane/rack board (#33), board layouts (#34).

If a venue cannot keep that rule, switch the nearest packs to time diversity.
